# MoMoCycle UG

MoMoCycle UG is a pioneering digital circular economy platform designed to solve urban waste accumulation and plastic pollution in Uganda. By bridging the gap between urban households, informal waste pickers, and recycling aggregators, the platform utilizes mobile apps and USSD channels to track recyclable recovery while deploying instant financial incentives directly via MTN MoMo.

## Problem Statement

Rapid urbanization across Uganda has drastically outpaced municipal waste management systems. Unmanaged solid waste—particularly plastic waste—clogs critical drainage channels, exacerbates urban flooding, threatens public health, and degrades local environments. Concurrently, informal waste pickers perform crucial environmental labor under precarious conditions with delayed, cash-based, and opaque compensation.

## The Solution: How MoMoCycle UG Works

* **Digital Tracking & Logistics:** Households and collectors log recyclable materials through an accessible smartphone interface or USSD shortcode.
* **Verified Aggregation:** Waste streams are weighed and routed to vetted local recycling aggregators.
* **Instant MTN MoMo Payouts:** Upon verification, automated fintech integration triggers instant micro-payments directly to the collector's mobile wallet, removing friction and financial delays.

## Impact & Value Proposition

* **Environmental:** Diverts tonnes of solid waste from landfills and stormwater drains, mitigating urban flooding and curbing carbon leakage.
* **Social:** Formalizes the informal waste sector, creating dignified green jobs and secure livelihoods for local youth.
* **Economic:** Directs immediate digital liquidity into grassroots communities, turning everyday waste management into a dependable micro-income source.

## Pilot Roadmap & Architecture

* **System Workflow:**
1. *Input:* User or collector interacts via USSD (`*165#` style) or app to log material type and weight.
2. *Verification:* Aggregator weighs physical drop-off and logs confirmation on the backend.
3. *Fintech Trigger:* Automated API trigger initiates an instant MTN MoMo transfer to the collector's mobile wallet.


* **Two-Week Testing Plan:**
* *Week 1:* Deploy USSD test environment, onboard an initial cohort of 10 youth waste collectors and 1 recycling aggregator.
* *Week 2:* Execute test waste logging, verify material weights, and run simulated or live MTN MoMo micro-payouts.



## Scalability & Growth Plan

By relying on existing mobile network coverage and MTN MoMo's ubiquitous financial architecture rather than heavy, fixed physical infrastructure, MoMoCycle UG is inherently asset-light. This allows rapid, cost-effective replication across major urban and peri-urban centers throughout Uganda as municipal waste pressures mount.
